About the Fellowship

Every year our program selects five extraordinary people with projects that combine technical rigor with social impact. Over 12 months, fellows receive personalized mentorship, access to expert networks, and funding to take their ideas from prototype to reality.

We believe the solutions to Latin America's greatest challenges are being built today by people like the ones featured on this page. Our job is to give them the support they need to scale.
